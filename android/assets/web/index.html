A cinema of your own.

Flow connects directly to your DhakaFlix server. No laptop, access key or account needed.

Stay on a Wi-Fi or ISP network that can reach 172.16.50.4. Movie posters are matched by title and year from IMDb, with Wikipedia/Wikimedia as a fallback. Server images are a last resort. Unavailable artwork gets a title card.

Tap fullscreen to rotate into landscape. Swipe up or down on the left half of the picture for brightness or on the right half for volume. Tap the three-dot menu for playback options.

Watchlist and progress stay on this device. Search covers your server catalog as indexing progresses. Playback uses bundled LibVLC hardware/software decoders inside Flow. Try Software mode if Auto cannot decode a file. Hardware performance still varies by device.

Online movie introductions come from Wikipedia lead paragraphs, with facts from Wikidata. No detailed Plot sections are requested. Sources can still mention story details; no summary service can guarantee every viewer’s spoiler preferences.
